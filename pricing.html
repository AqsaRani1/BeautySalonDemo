Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I pause my membership?
Yes — all memberships can be paused for up to 2 months per year, for any reason including holidays, illness, or new babies. Simply contact us at least 7 days before your next billing date and we'll handle the rest.
Do unused treatments roll over?
Bloom and Radiance treatments roll over for one month if you miss them. After that they expire. Elite members can roll over up to 3 treatments in any given month. We recommend booking in advance to avoid losing any treatments.
Can I share my membership with a partner?
Memberships are individual and non-transferable. However, we offer a Couples Membership add-on at a 15% discount — please call us to discuss your options. Our team will find the most cost-effective arrangement for you.
What is your cancellation policy?
We ask for 24 hours' notice for appointment cancellations. Cancellations within 24 hours or no-shows will use one of your monthly treatment credits. We understand that emergencies happen and will always consider exceptional circumstances.
Are gift cards available?
Absolutely. Lumière gift cards are available in any denomination from £25 to £500. They can be purchased in person, by phone, or via our website and are valid for 12 months. Physical cards with beautiful botanical packaging are available at no extra cost.
Is there a joining fee?
There is no joining fee for Bloom or Radiance memberships. The Elite membership has a one-time £50 onboarding fee which includes your first dedicated therapist consultation, a full skin analysis, and a personalised treatment plan mapped out for the year ahead.
